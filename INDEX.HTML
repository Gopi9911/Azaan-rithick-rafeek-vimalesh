Empowering Education in the Hills

Government Arts and Science College, Coonoor is a premier higher education institution situated amidst the serene and picturesque Nilgiris hills of Tamil Nadu.

Affiliated with Bharathiar University, Coimbatore, the institution strives to maintain excellence in teaching, learning, and skill development in a tranquil campus setting.

Dedicated to supporting rural and regional youth for competitive career growth.
